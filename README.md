# Freebies — Free Small-Business Tools

A no-nonsense directory of free tools for invoicing, bookkeeping, scheduling,
and contracts — built for small businesses, authors, and resellers (eBay/Amazon,
KDP publishing). Every listing shows cost, platform, steps to get started, and
the tool's official link.

**Published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana) — the small-business and
author-tools side of Dylan Asdale's work (author of *The Massive AI Manual*;
hub site: dylanasdale.github.io).

**No affiliate links.** Every link goes to the tool's official website.

## How to use

Pick a category, then pick a tool. Each listing includes the cost, supported
platforms, short steps to get started, and a link to the official site. Costs
and plan limits change over time — check each official site before signing up.
Listings are educational and are not legal, tax, or medical advice.

## Invoicing

### Wave Invoicing
- **Cost:** Free — unlimited invoices; fees only if you accept online payments
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Create a free Wave account.
  2. Add your business name, address, and logo.
  3. Create a new invoice, add your customer and line items.
  4. Send by email or share a payment link.
  5. Mark invoices as paid and view reports in your dashboard.
- **Official link:** https://www.waveapps.com/invoicing

### Zoho Invoice
- **Cost:** Free for small businesses; unlimited invoices
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign up for a free Zoho Invoice account.
  2. Set up your organization and invoice template.
  3. Add a customer.
  4. Create and send an invoice (or estimate).
  5. Track payment status and send reminders.
- **Official link:** https://www.zoho.com/invoice/

### Invoice Ninja
- **Cost:** Free to self-host; paid cloud plans
- **Platform:** Web; self-host (Docker)
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign up for the cloud free tier, or self-host with Docker.
  2. Add your company details and logo.
  3. Create a client record.
  4. Create an invoice and send it.
  5. Record payments when received.
- **Official link:** https://invoiceninja.com/

### Square Invoices
- **Cost:** Free to send; processing fees apply to paid invoices
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign up for a Square account.
  2. Go to Invoices and create an invoice.
  3. Add your customer, items, and due date.
  4. Send by email or text message.
  5. Track payments in your Square Dashboard.
- **Official link:** https://squareup.com/invoices

### PayPal Invoicing
- **Cost:** Free to create and send; payment processing fees apply
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Log in to PayPal and go to Invoicing.
  2. Click Create invoice.
  3. Add your customer and items; set tax or discount.
  4. Preview and send the invoice.
  5. Track payments in Activity.
- **Official link:** https://www.paypal.com/us/invoice

## Bookkeeping

### Wave Accounting
- **Cost:** Free accounting; fees for payments and payroll add-ons
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Create a free Wave account.
  2. Set up your chart of accounts (or keep the defaults).
  3. Import transactions or connect your bank (optional).
  4. Categorize income and expenses.
  5. Run Profit & Loss and other reports.
- **Official link:** https://www.waveapps.com/

### GnuCash
- **Cost:** Free, open source
- **Platform:** Windows, Mac, Linux
- **Steps:**
  1. Download and install GnuCash for your system.
  2. Create a new file and set up your accounts.
  3. Enter income and expenses as transactions.
  4. Reconcile accounts against your statements.
  5. Run reports such as Profit & Loss.
- **Official link:** https://www.gnucash.org/

### Manager.io
- **Cost:** Free desktop edition (one business); cloud edition is paid
- **Platform:** Windows, Mac, Linux
- **Steps:**
  1. Download the free desktop edition.
  2. Create a new business file.
  3. Set up your chart of accounts and tax codes.
  4. Record sales, purchases, and payments.
  5. Generate financial reports.
- **Official link:** https://www.manager.io/

### Google Sheets
- **Cost:** Free with a Google account
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Open Google Sheets and start a new spreadsheet (or choose a budget template).
  2. Create columns for date, description, category, income, and expense.
  3. Log each transaction.
  4. Use SUM formulas to total by category.
  5. Reconcile against your bank statement monthly.
- **Official link:** https://sheets.google.com/

### NCH Express Accounts
- **Cost:** Free for businesses with fewer than five employees
- **Platform:** Windows, Mac
- **Steps:**
  1. Download and install the free edition.
  2. Set up your company and accounts.
  3. Enter sales, payments, and expenses.
  4. Run financial reports.
  5. Back up your data file regularly.
- **Official link:** https://www.nchsoftware.com/accounts/

## Scheduling

### Calendly
- **Cost:** Free tier (one event type); paid plans add more
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Create a free Calendly account.
  2. Connect your calendar (Google, Outlook, etc.).
  3. Set your availability hours.
  4. Create an event type (for example, consultation).
  5. Share your booking link with clients.
- **Official link:** https://calendly.com/

### Setmore
- **Cost:** Free tier available; paid plans for more staff and features
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign up for a free Setmore account.
  2. Add your services and staff.
  3. Set your business hours and availability.
  4. Customize your booking page.
  5. Share the booking link or embed it on your site.
- **Official link:** https://www.setmore.com/

### Google Calendar
- **Cost:** Free with a Google account
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Open Google Calendar.
  2. Create an event and add your client as a guest.
  3. Set the date, time, and reminders.
  4. Add a video call or location (optional).
  5. Save and send the invitation.
- **Official link:** https://calendar.google.com/

### SimplyMeet
- **Cost:** Free tier available; paid plans for more event types
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Create a free SimplyMeet account.
  2. Connect your calendar.
  3. Set your availability.
  4. Create a meeting type.
  5. Share your booking link.
- **Official link:** https://simplymeet.me/

## Contracts

### Dropbox Sign
- **Cost:** Free tier: 3 signature requests per month; paid plans for more
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Create a free Dropbox Sign account.
  2. Upload your contract.
  3. Add signature and date fields.
  4. Add signer email addresses.
  5. Send for signature and track status.
- **Official link:** https://www.dropbox.com/sign

### PandaDoc
- **Cost:** Free tier available; paid plans for advanced features
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Create a free PandaDoc account.
  2. Upload a contract or choose a template.
  3. Add signature, date, and text fields.
  4. Send to the client for signature.
  5. Track when it is opened and signed.
- **Official link:** https://www.pandadoc.com/

### Jotform Sign
- **Cost:** Free tier available (Starter: 5 forms / 100 submissions per month)
- **Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Steps:**
  1. Create a free Jotform account.
  2. Build a document or upload a PDF.
  3. Add signature fields.
  4. Publish and share the signing link.
  5. Collect and download signed copies.
- **Official link:** https://www.jotform.com/sign/

### SignWell
- **Cost:** Free tier: 3 documents per month; paid plans for unlimited
- **Platform:** Web
- **Steps:**
  1. Create a free SignWell account.
  2. Upload your document.
  3. Add signers and signature fields.
  4. Send for signature.
  5. Download the executed contract and audit trail.
- **Official link:** https://signwell.com/

### Signaturely
- **Cost:** Free tier: 3 signature requests per month
- **Platform:** Web
- **Steps:**
  1. Create a free Signaturely account.
  2. Upload your contract.
  3. Add signature and date fields and signers.
  4. Send for signature.
  5. Store and download the completed document.
- **Official link:** https://www.signaturely.com/
